Nubia Muñoz Calero Formation School

Our formation school is the gateway to becoming a full member of WIE UIS. Through it, applicants explore the history of women in STEM, develop critical thinking skills, and build a deeper understanding of the challenges and achievements of women in engineering and science. It is a space for reflection, learning, and community.
