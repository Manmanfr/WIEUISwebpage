Who is WIE UIS?

We are an IEEE affinity group and a student chapter that focuses in encouraging women from Santander to study carreers in STEM fields as well as supporting the women who already are.

For us at WIE UIS it's important to take into consideration our colombian context, where having access to college education is a privilege on itself.

Key aspects of WIE UIS

A Subheading

WIE UIS is based in Santander, Colombia. This makes our approach to the struggles and opportunities woman suffer in STEM fields different, since we have to take into consideration some extra factors.

What are we doing at WIE UIS?

Ingeniamujer Week

Trayectorias con impacto

  • 09-03-2026

This year we started with a new series of activities that we chose to call "Ingeniamujer: Trayectorias con impacto". These activities were held the week before international woman's day, they consisted of different activites such as talks with women experts on fields like geology, power electronics and optical physics, as well as guided visits to laboratories, garage sales, film screenings, and many more!
