Get to know us!

Who is WIE UIS?

We are an IEEE affinity group and a student chapter that focuses in encouraging women from Santander to study carreers in STEM fields as well as supporting the women who already are.

For us at WIE UIS it's important to take into consideration our colombian context, where having access to college education is a privilege on itself.

Key aspects of WIE UIS

Multidisciplinarity

At WIE UIS we believe that multidisciplinarity is important for us to create an academic community. That's why our members come from different academic fields, such as: Electronic engineering, industrial engneering, metallugical engineering, computer science and chemistry.

Extension projects

The academy shouldn't stop at the college's walls, if we want to create a real change in our society we need to share our knowledge and motivate others to join our path. That's why we have projects that involve motivating woman and girls who don't belong to UIS' community. These projects are mainly focused at public schools in Santander, this is so we can help in closing the gap in accesing to college education in Colombia.

Critical thinking

Through our formation school Nubia Muñoz Calero we encourage critical thinking in the applicants for our student chapter, helping them in understanding and questioning the struggles that women have gone through in STEM fields and their working enviroments.

LGBTQ+ friendly

As WIE UIS we recognize the overlapping in the problems of women and LGBT+ people and we believe that intersectionality is important when taking them into consideration. That's why we believe that we need to support each other. WIE UIS is the perfect space for discussing and understanding the roots of these problems and the possible solutions to them.

Ingeniamujer Week

Trayectorias con impacto

  • 09-03-2026

This year we started with a new series of activities that we chose to call "Ingeniamujer: Trayectorias con impacto". These activities were held the week before international woman's day, they consisted of different activites such as talks with women experts on fields like geology, power electronics and optical physics, as well as guided visits to laboratories, garage sales, film screenings, and many more!
